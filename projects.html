End-to-end Process Automation & Reporting Suite

The Challenge

A critical business process was manual, in a risk to human error, and took 40 hours per month to complete. Data was across multiple Excel sheets.

The Solution

Engineered a custom solution using Python and Excel VBA to scrape/clean data automatically. I then integrated this into Power Automate to trigger notifications and updates across the team.

The Result

Reduced manual processing time by 95% and eliminated data entry errors, saving the department approximately 38 hours per month.

Operational Throughput Optimization

The Challenge

Identified a major bottleneck in the purchase department workflow that was causing a 4 day delay in delivery/completion.

The Solution

Applied DMAIC methodology to identify root causes. I redesigned the workflow to remove redundant steps and implemented a Power BI dashboard to provide real-time visibility into the "health" of the process.

The Result

Increased process throughput by 80% and reduced lead time by 3 days, directly improving customer/stakeholder satisfaction.

Advanced Predictive Analytics & KPI Dashboard The Challenge

Management lacked clear visibility into historical data trends, making it difficult to make proactive, data-driven decisions.

The Solution
